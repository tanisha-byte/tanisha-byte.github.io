cat about.md

by day i'm a developer relations engineer at bolna (yc f25), the voice ai platform. i built the bolna mcp server and the bolna cli from scratch, got bolna listed on claude, openai, composio, make, n8n and zapier, restructured the docs, run the blog, and started bolna builders and campus builders.

before bolna i was the ai developer relations engineer at suprsend, owning the developer journey end-to-end - first api call to production - through demos, workshops, docs, sample apps, video tutorials, and conference keynotes. before suprsend i was a software engineer on the azure core compute team at microsoft, working on region-aware configuration across 12+ azure regions and being dri for high-severity incidents that touched 2m+ users.

before that, i was an ai product engineer at digital product school (sap labs munich), where i led a product pivot and shipped an ai sales-forecasting platform that improved forecast accuracy by 30%.

outside of devrel i wear a few other hats. i'm the founder of project hitl, a community for the people working at the seam between ai systems and the humans who supervise, correct, or evaluate them. i hold a private pilot license (garg aviation academy, 2021), learning to dj on weekends (still very much a student), and crochet on most evenings (the wip pile is embarrassing). i'm also a faeb scholar on the arena technical ai safety fellowship (nov-dec 2025).

most of my technical writing lives on hashnode (@tansgrid) - suprsend integration guides, microsoft internship recaps, side-project tutorials. my substack is the more personal space - essays, reflections, the bits that don't fit in a tutorial. linkedin (~10,000 followers) is where most of the dev-community conversation happens.

on the docs side: i wrote roughly 90% of the suprsend developer documentation - quickstarts, sdk integrations, integration playbooks for segment, twilio, sendgrid, aws sns, and the go-live checklist. it's the work i'm quietly proudest of.

i've also built a stack of demo apps for customers, workshops, and conference talks - from suprsend integration starters to a cursor / claude code agent skill for content creators, a docusign integration demo, and the holospeak smart india hackathon finalist project. currently learning ml ops on the side.

i've given 53+ talks, keynotes and workshops - most recently two sessions at mcp dev summit seoul, scale 23x in pasadena, and a voice-ai testing workshop at tais manila. and when i'm not shipping devtools, i'm co-founder of chae, a matcha brand.
